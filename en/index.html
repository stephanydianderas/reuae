▸ El mercado

Why the UAE is still
one of the best markets
in the world to invest in

◈

0% income tax

Rental income and capital gains on sales are not taxed for individuals. A structural tax advantage few markets can match.

◈

Reference gross yields

2025 averages in Dubai: around 7% gross for apartments and 5% for villas (Cavendish Maxwell). Net yield depends on the area and costs.

◈

Residency linked to property

Buying property can give access to residence visas, including the 10-year Golden Visa. Requirements have changed recently: confirm them with the Dubai Land Department before investing.

◈

A regulated, transparent market

The Dubai Land Department (DLD) offers open data on real estate transactions. Historical prices and volume can be checked against official data.

◈

Sustained international demand

Dubai welcomed 18.72 million international visitors in 2024 (Dubai DET). Rental demand remains backed by a growing expatriate base.

◈

Massive infrastructure projects

From the Dubai Metro to the new districts of Ras Al Khaimah and Sharjah, public infrastructure investment keeps lifting emerging areas.
